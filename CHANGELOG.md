# Change log

MONOPOLITBURO is in beta. Rules changes bump the minor version; wording and layout fixes bump the patch.

## v0.2.0 (October 2026)

After two playtests.

### Rules
- Denunciation is once per **round**, not once per Year. The Seksot gets two; the Secretary gets one free.
- Refusing an Inspection bribe costs **+2** Suspicion. At year end the richest citizen takes +2 and the second richest +1.
- Appointments and auction wins cost **20 extra per point** of the buyer's Suspicion.
- New ways to shed Suspicion: Subbotnik (pay 100 to drop 1), a denunciation that sends someone to the Gulag (drop 1), visiting the Gulag corner (drop 1).
- Auctions spelled out: open at 10, bids in 10s, the Secretary never bids and keeps 30.
- The Secretary's powers and limits are written down. The District Fund is public.
- Quotas raised (4–5 players: 300 / 500 / 700 / 900). Kulak Line lowered from 4,000 to **3,000**.
- A Secretary who misses the Year 4 quota hands the win to the richest citizen.
- Red Army Officer: free railways and 1 Blat, but +1 Suspicion whenever any citizen is disappeared. The Year 3 penalty is gone.

### Components
- All amounts rounded to 10s; the 1 and 5 notes are retired.
- Deeds show their purchase price and pledge / redeem values.
- Aids are single-sided. Suspicion trackers are separate cards. The Year track is a board sticker.
- Black glass beads replace the house and hotel markers.
- Every piece carries its version number.
